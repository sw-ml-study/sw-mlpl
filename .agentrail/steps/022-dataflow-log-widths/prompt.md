Dataflow renderer log-width channel (../emufpga ask): add opt-in width_scale on the edges record -- "linear" (default) | "log". Log-normalizes each edges.widths value into a sane pixel band by natural log so extreme ratios (e.g. 269e6 vs 4096 bytes, 65000:1) render as an honest orders-of-magnitude contrast instead of blowing out the canvas or vanishing. Linear stays default and gains a safety clamp so a raw quantity can't become a monstrous stroke-width.
